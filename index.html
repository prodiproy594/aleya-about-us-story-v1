ABOUT US

Aleya — a name inspired by elevation, elegance, and timeless presence.

Founded in Adelaide, Australia, Aleya draws inspiration from the enduring sophistication of European winter fashion, reimagined through a modern lens of luxury, warmth, and effortless style.

Each piece is thoughtfully designed to evoke a sense of confidence, presence, and refinement. From luxurious furs and soft luxury lining to considered functionality and timeless silhouettes, every coat is crafted to balance comfort, sophistication, and enduring elegance.

Produced in limited quantities, Aleya collections are intentionally exclusive, creating pieces that feel distinctive, considered, and timeless.

“Aleya exists for those who appreciate timeless elegance, exceptional comfort, and effortless sophistication.”

BEHIND THE BRAND

The Founder Story

Growing up in Europe, fur coats were a symbol of elegance and confidence. After moving to Australia eight years ago, I found myself struggling to find a fur coat that felt truly luxurious, warm, and timeless.

I began designing my own pieces through carefully selected manufacturers, creating coats I genuinely wanted to wear across every occasion. The coats quickly drew attention wherever they were worn, with women constantly asking where they were from.

That moment became Aleya.

Aleya is a luxury fur coat brand designed to make you feel unforgettable. Every piece in our collection comes in a limited stock.
